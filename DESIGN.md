## Overview

Savora Trading Atelier is a light-first, gallery-like system. Monumental word forms, framed photographs, circular editorial stamps, fine rules, and numbered chapters make the site feel commissioned rather than templated. The tone combines a cultural food journal with disciplined commercial communication.

## Colors

Warm ivory is the primary canvas. Midnight navy creates concentrated narrative chapters rather than dominating every section. Olive carries the natural-food connection and muted gold appears as framing, directional marks, and restrained emphasis.

## Typography

Bodoni Moda is used at monumental scale for names, chapter statements, quotes, and category titles. Its sharper contrast gives the composition a more contemporary luxury character. Manrope carries factual copy, indexed navigation, captions, forms, and commercial detail with cleaner geometry and more relaxed readability.

## Layout

The sticky ivory header keeps the primary navigation and enquiry action available on desktop. The full-bleed image and headline fill the opening screen beneath it. Shared page gutters align the company story, product selector, service disclosures and enquiry form. Product detail and service content use short entrance transitions with reduced-motion support.

The homepage is organized as a sequence of numbered chapters. The opening screen is a simple full-bleed photographic statement with one clearly anchored text block; it avoids collage overlap and lets visitors understand the brand immediately. Later sections alternate offset images, long editorial copy, structured timelines, an interactive product triptych, and circular service diagrams.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from clipped image reveals, physical-looking gold frame offsets, overlapping type, orbital lines, and collage-like placement. Motion is calm: draw-on lines, gentle scale changes, staggered reveals, and continuous typographic bands.

## Shapes

Controls use pill geometry; content panels use restrained 18px rounding. Curves are directional, not decorative flourishes.

## Components

Product exploration uses a three-part selector: indexed categories, changing imagery, and commercial detail. Story and service sections use numbered chapters instead of cards. The page tracks scroll progress with an olive rule, reveals content through IntersectionObserver, and respects reduced-motion preferences.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do use generous space, authentic food textures, and precise typographic hierarchy. Do not use neon color, bright gradients, excessive glassmorphism, or altered versions of the official mark.
